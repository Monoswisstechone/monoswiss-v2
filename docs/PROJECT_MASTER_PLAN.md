## 1. Project Vision & Goals

**Vision:** To create a modern, professional, and high-performance marketing website that showcases Monoswiss Technology Ltd.'s expertise and drives business growth.

**Goals:**
-   Establish a strong online presence that reflects the company's brand and capabilities.
-   Generate qualified leads through a clear user journey and an effective contact form.
-   Serve as a hub for company content, including portfolio case studies and blog articles.
-   Achieve high performance, accessibility, and SEO rankings.

---

## 2. Project Phases

The project is structured into distinct phases to ensure organized development and clear deliverables.

| Phase | Title | Status | Key Deliverables |
| :---- | :---- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Initial Development & Refactoring** | ✅ **Completed** | - Functional single-page website <br> - Responsive design implementation <br> - JavaScript interactivity <br> - Code quality refactoring |
| 2 | **Production Readiness (Baseline)** | ✅ **Completed** | - SEO & Social Media Metadata <br> - Favicon, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` <br> - Validated HTML (no duplicate IDs) |
| 3 | **Content Migration & Asset Integration** | ⏸️ **Pending** | - Replacement of all placeholder text <br> - Integration of final brand assets (logo, images) <br> - `CONTENT_LIBRARY.md` finalized |
| 4 | **Backend & Functionality** | ⏸️ **Pending** | - Functional contact form backend <br> - Internal page creation (Case Studies, Blog) |
| 5 | **QA, Accessibility & Performance Audit** | ⏸️ **Pending** | - Final accessibility review (WCAG compliance) <br> - Performance optimization (image compression, minification) |
| 6 | **Deployment & Launch** | ⏸️ **Pending** | - Production deployment to hosting provider <br> - DNS configuration and go-live |
